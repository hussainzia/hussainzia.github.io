01 · About me

Engineering a smarter tomorrow

I'm Hussain Zia, a Mechatronics Engineering student from Karachi, Pakistan. I'm drawn to the point where hardware meets software: programming the electronics that read sensors, make decisions and drive motors, and putting them together into robots and automated systems.

Alongside engineering, I build software. I study modern AI with Python at PIAIC and web development at Aptech, which helps me add data, intelligence and clean interfaces to the machines I design.
